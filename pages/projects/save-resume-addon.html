The Challenge

Complex multi-page forms (mortgage inquiries, university applications, insurance audits) experience up to 68% drop-off rates when applicants cannot pause and resume their submission across devices. Storing incomplete submissions in cleartext violates privacy compliance (GDPR / HIPAA) and creates significant liability.

The Solution

I engineered a cryptographically secured state-machine addon for WPForms. Form values are serialized, signed with an HMAC digest, and encrypted using AES-256-GCM before storage. A single-use authenticated magic link is emailed to the user, allowing one-click resumption from any device without requiring a WordPress user account.
